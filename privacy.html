Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Last updated October 10, 2026

Wagtiv is a dog-walking game for iPhone made by Kellen Blake, an individual developer based in North Carolina, USA ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information Wagtiv collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email kellenblake3@gmail.com.

The short version: Wagtiv uses your location to record your walks and run the game. Your walk routes are private to your account. Your dog's name, photo, breed (if you add one), level, totals, and (if you choose) your U.S. state appear on leaderboards that other Wagtiv users can see. There are no ads, I don't sell your data, and I don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app.

1. Information Wagtiv collects

Account information

When you create an account, Wagtiv stores your email address and how you signed in (Apple, Google, or email and password). If you sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email, in which case Wagtiv receives an Apple relay address instead; Apple may also share your name if you allow it. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile information (such as your name and profile picture link), which the sign-in service stores with your account.

If you sign up with an email address and password, your password is stored only in hashed (scrambled) form by the sign-in service; I never see it. Wagtiv emails you a one-time code to confirm your address when you sign up, and another if you ask to reset your password. These emails come from Wagtiv <auth@4926creativecollect.com>, a domain I own and use to send Wagtiv's emails; replies to them reach me at kellenblake3@gmail.com.

Your dogs

Each dog's name, optional photo, optional breed, optional birthday, and game progress (level, experience points, and bones collected).

Location

Wagtiv uses your device's precise location:

  • During a walk, to record your route, measure distance and time, and place and find bones on the map. If you give Wagtiv "Always" location permission, it keeps recording an active walk while the app is in the background or your screen is locked. Wagtiv doesn't track your location in the background when you're not on a walk.
  • On the Home screen, to show the current weather where you are (see "Weather" below).

For each completed walk, Wagtiv saves the route (a series of GPS coordinates), start and end times, distance, bones found, and experience earned. Walk routes are visible only to you.

You can turn location access off or change it at any time in iOS Settings. Without location, walks can't be recorded.

Photos

If you choose to, you can add a photo of your dog and an end-of-walk photo, taken with your camera or picked from your library. Wagtiv only accesses the photo you select. Photos are resized before upload. They are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses that are not password-protected, so anyone who has a photo's exact link could view it. Dog photos are shown to other users on leaderboards; end-of-walk photos aren't shown to other users anywhere in the app.

Your state (optional)

If you set your U.S. state, it's used to place your dogs on the state leaderboard.

Apple Health (optional)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Wagtiv reads two things from it: the number of steps you took during each walk, and, if you wore an Apple Watch, your average heart rate during the walk. Wagtiv only reads data for the time of your walks, never writes anything to Apple Health, and doesn't read any other health data.

These numbers are shown in your walk summary and stats, and saved with each walk in your account so you can see them later. They are never shown to other users or on leaderboards, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with anyone except the hosting provider that stores your account (see "Service providers"). Health information is sensitive, and Wagtiv only uses it to show you your own walk stats.

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and Wagtiv works the same without it. You can turn access off anytime in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wagtiv. Turning access off stops new readings; the steps and heart rate already saved with past walks are deleted when you delete those walks, the dog, or your account.

Reports

If you report another dog's name, breed, or photo on a leaderboard, Wagtiv stores the report: your account ID, the reported dog, the reason you chose, and when you reported it. I receive an email about each report so I can review it. The person you report isn't told who reported them. Dogs you hide from your leaderboard are remembered only on your device.

Information kept only on your device

Wagtiv stores some data only on your iPhone: an in-progress walk (so it can be resumed if the app closes), recent bone locations (so bones don't respawn in the same spots right away), and your app preferences. During a walk, Wagtiv can show your walk stats in a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island; that data stays on your device.

What Wagtiv doesn't collect

Wagtiv doesn't include advertising, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. It doesn't access your contacts or microphone, or any health data beyond the optional Apple Health steps and heart rate described above.

2. How your information is used

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in, including emailing you one-time codes to verify your email or reset your password. Wagtiv doesn't send marketing emails.
  • To record walks, run the game (bones, experience, levels, streaks), and show your history and stats, including your steps and heart rate if you connect Apple Health.
  • To show leaderboards, and to review reports of inappropriate content.
  • To show local weather.
  • To respond when you contact me, and to maintain, fix, and improve the app.

I don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and I don't use it to profile you for advertising.

3. What other users can see

Leaderboards are visible to signed-in Wagtiv users and show each dog's name, photo, breed (if set), level, bones or distance total, rank, and the owner's U.S. state (if set). Leaderboards never show your email, your name, your walk routes, your location, or any health data. Please don't put personal information in your dog's name, breed, or photo.

5. Retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account in the app at any time: Profile → Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, dogs, walks, photos, and any reports you've filed from Wagtiv's database and storage, and clears Wagtiv's data from your device. Copies in the hosting provider's backups may persist for a limited time until those backups expire. You can also request deletion by emailing kellenblake3@gmail.com from the address on your account.

Deleting a single dog in the app deletes that dog, its walk history, and its profile photo.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location, camera, and photos: change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Leaderboard state: you can leave it blank.
  • Breed: you can leave it blank.
  • Access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data: most of it can be viewed and edited in the app. For anything else, email me and I'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights under local law. I'll honor those requests too. I won't treat you differently for exercising them.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each account can read and change only its own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

Wagtiv isn't directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email me and I'll delete it.

9. International users

Wagtiv is operated from the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the effective date above. For material changes, I'll also give notice in the app or by email before they take effect.
